Supporting · 2019
“There are a lot of thoughtful people who are busy and can't always make it to a town hall or city council meeting. But now they can lend their voices in a civil, organized way.”
Nick Mastronardi · Polco / NRC · 2019

The insight, in Nick’s words, is about public goods. Private markets have competition. Local government mostly does not. A resident can challenge a decision and still lack the data a company would take for granted.

He frames Polco as a Bloomberg terminal for local government — civil, verified resident voice, not Facebook noise, not a SurveyMonkey link anyone can stuff. On Aug 26 he put a scale on that metaphor: about 400 U.S. local governments as customers, pointed at about 40,000 local jurisdictions, with about 690 performance indicators going back roughly 15 years. Those are his numbers from the room. This page does not recarve them as a press kit.

Founding proof, still uncarved: an accelerator, three customers in three months. Later: about 400 governments, slowly coming onto the floor. No Deloitte claim. No Bloomberg Philanthropy claim. No merger, LOI, or SAFE.

Polco, as Nick frames it, is that terminal: about 400 U.S. local governments already using it, aimed at the much larger field of local jurisdictions, with a long performance-indicator history behind the product. Soft language. Not a carved tally.
