The same government record. A harder question.

Who actually writes the law?

People’s Lobby is a white-label of Citizen Portal. Same record. Different question: the rise of corporate power in American government, and the lessening of everyday people’s influence. Not a PAC. Not a talk show. The filings.

You already know what a Formula 1 jacket looks like. Every patch is a sponsor. Now picture a sitting member wearing their biggest donors the same way. That picture is not a stunt. It is the disclosure, finally arranged so a person can see it.
